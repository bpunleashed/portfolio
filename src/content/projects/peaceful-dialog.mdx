story_en: |
  In the bite-sized stop-motion short Peaceful Dialog, one can trace several echoes of Nick Park’s animated classics in both form and content. At the same time, the claymation visuals, cacophonic language, and bizarre sketch-like absurdity feel straight out of the point-and-click adventure game The Neverhood or Amanita Design’s Czech gem Machinarium. While this episodic novelty may feel plucked from a larger whole, its craftsmanship, attention to detail, and brisk editing are beyond reproach. If one were determined to look for a deeper message, it could be summed up in a tweaked cliché: The word is mightier than the sword, but sometimes a materialized train or laser vision doesn't hurt either.
  
  - Pavel Knapko, Forbes editor
